Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should a paver patio base be dug?
For pedestrian walkways and residential patios, excavate 7 to 8 inches deep (4 inches of compacted #21A crushed stone base, 1 inch of bedding sand, and 2-3/8 inches of paver thickness). For vehicular driveways, increase crushed stone base to 8 to 10 inches.
Why should bedding sand never be compacted before laying pavers?
Bedding sand must remain loose and uncompacted when screeded. Laying pavers directly onto screeded sand and then running a plate compactor over the finished pavers locks the sand upward into the paver bevel joints from below.
What is the 20% gravel compaction factor?
Loose crushed aggregate contains significant air pockets. When compacted with a heavy mechanical gas plate compactor, volume decreases by 15% to 20%. Failing to order extra gravel results in a base that is significantly too thin.
